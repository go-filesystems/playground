go-filesystems playground

Drop a disk image. The page says which filesystem it is and lets you browse it, view small files and download any file. Everything runs in your browser, in WebAssembly: nothing is uploaded.

Drop an image here or or

Detected: FAT32, exFAT, ext4, NTFS, ISO 9660, SquashFS, HFS+ and UFS at offset 0. APFS, Btrfs, XFS and ZFS are found in a disk image's partition table, or tried in turn when nothing is detected; choose one to force it. An encrypted image, LUKS1, LUKS2 or an APFS FileVault volume, asks for its password; the filesystem inside is then detected, or the one chosen.

The password goes to the engine in this tab, for this open only. It is not stored and not sent anywhere.

Select a file to view it. Text up to 1 MiB and images up to 8 MiB are shown here; any file can be downloaded.
